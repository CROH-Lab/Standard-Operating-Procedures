# Standard protocol for characterizing sediment composition through controlled bruns

### Prepared by: Coral Reef and Ocean Health Research Group
### Last updated: July, 2023
### Pre-requisite SOP: NA
### Safety precautions: **Required PPE - <ins> enclosed shoes, gloves </ins>**

## Purpose: 
The purpose of this procedure is to analyze the composition of sediment based on weight differences after a series of burns. 

### Materials:
1. Spoons
2. Brushes
3. Petri dish/aluminum weigh boat
4. Glass vials
5. Ceramic crucibles
6. Desiccator

## Equipment:
1. Electronic balance (resolution of 0.0001g)
2. Drying oven
3. Muffle furnace
4. Sediment grinder

### Preparation:
1. Collect 3-4 spoonful of sediment and spread out in a petri dish to dry (~2-4 weeks or 2-4 days in an oven at 50˚C). Be sure to take a representative subsample from that site (there may be some rubble on the bottom of bag, etc.).
2. Once dry, grind sediment to a fine powder with grinder. Place ground samples into glass vials labeled with site name. Ensure glass vials are full of ground sample (~25 - 30g of sediment).
3. Weigh ceramic crucibles and record weights. Record which crucible number corresponds to each sample in notebook for proper identification (crucibles cannot be labeled as Sharpie, etc. will burn off).
