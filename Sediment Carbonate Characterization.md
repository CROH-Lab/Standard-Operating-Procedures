# Standard protocol for characterizing sediment composition through controlled bruns

### Prepared by: Coral Reef and Ocean Health Research Group
### Last updated: July, 2023
### Pre-requisite SOP: NA
### Safety precautions: **Required PPE - <ins> enclosed shoes, gloves </ins>**

## Purpose: 
The purpose of this procedure is to analyze the composition of sediment based on weight differences after a series of burns. 

### Materials:
1. Spoons
2. Brushes
3. Petri dish/aluminum weigh boat
4. Glass vials
5. Ceramic crucibles
6. Desiccator

## Equipment:
1. Electronic balance (resolution of 0.0001g)
2. Drying oven
3. Muffle furnace
4. Sediment grinder

### Preparation:
1. Collect 3-4 spoonful of sediment and spread out in a petri dish to dry (~2-4 weeks or 2-4 days in an oven at 50˚C). Be sure to take a representative subsample from that site (there may be some rubble on the bottom of bag, etc.).
2. Once dry, grind sediment to a fine powder with grinder. Place ground samples into glass vials labeled with site name. Ensure glass vials are full of ground sample (~25 - 30g of sediment).
3. Weigh ceramic crucibles and record weights. Record which crucible number corresponds to each sample in notebook for proper identification (crucibles cannot be labeled as Sharpie, etc. will burn off).

### *Compositoin Burns*
- 100°C for 10 hours
  - Use drying oven
  - After 10 hours at 100°C,  place crucibles into desiccator with vent overnight Cool overnight in the oven. Once cool, weigh crucibles and record weights (removal of all moisture)
- 500°C for 12 hours
  - Use muffle furnace. Place crucibles inside before turning on, but allow at least 45 minutes for furnace to reach temperature before starting 12-hr timer.
  - After 12 hours at 500°C, use gloves and tongs to transfer crucibles to desiccator with vent overnight Cool overnight in the muffle furnace or until furnace is cool.
  - Once cool, weigh crucibles and record weights (removal of organic fraction)
- 1,000°C for 2 hours
  - Use muffle furnace. Place crucibles inside before turning on but allow at least 1-1 ½ hours for furnace to reach temperature before starting 2-hr timer.
  - After 2 hours at 1,000°C, use tongs to transfer crucibles to desiccator with vent overnight. Cool overnight in the muffle furnace or until sample has cooled down (may be longer than overnight). Be very careful when removing from furnace. **_Even when using tongs, arm hair can burn off_**.
  - Once cool, weigh crucibles and record weights (removal of carbonate fraction)
- Calculate percent organic, carbonate, and terrigenous fractions from weight differences between burns
  - The sediment remaining is of terrigenous origin
  - *Note: do not need to conduct multiple weighting of crucibles because desiccator keeps moisture out*
