<a name = "Materials"></a> 
### <ins>Materials</ins>
1. Cultures in 15 µL falcon tubes (received from NOAA)
  - TIso - _TIsochrysis lutea_
  - Mono - _Diacronema lutheri_
  - TW - _Thalassiosira weissflogii_
  - Ply-429 - _Tetraselmis chui_
  - VA-19 - _Nannochloris oculata_
  - Syn - _Synechococcus bacillaris_ 

2. Autoclaved test tubes with algae stock solution, labelled
3. Ethanol burner
4. Artificial seawater (salinity level variable)
5. 20L carboy
6. Fritz Pro Algae Food Part A
7. Fritz Pro Algae Food Part B
8. Sodium silicate powder
9. 1L beaker
10. Stir bar

<a name = "Equipment"></a> 
### <ins>Equipment</ins>
1. Vortexer, Four E’s Scientific
2. Scale
3. 5L graduated cylinder
4. Stir plate
