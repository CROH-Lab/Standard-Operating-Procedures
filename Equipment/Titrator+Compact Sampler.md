# Titrator and COmpact Sampler SOP

### Prepared by: Coral Reef and Ocean Health Research Group
### Last updated: February 5, 2025
### Pre-requisite SOP: [Titrator SOP](Equipment/Titrator.md)
### Safety precautions: **Required PPE - <ins> gloves, closed-toe shoes </ins>**

## Purpose: 
The purpose of this SOP is to help the user properly measure the pH. 

### Equipment:
1. Titrator (Metrohm, EcoTitrator)
2. Compact Sample Changer (Metrohm)
3. Water Bath (VWR)
4. Scale (VWR, VWR-224AC)
5. 100 mL Pipette (VWR)
6. Water aerator

### Maintenance of the pH Probe and Titrator
Reference [Titrator SOP](Equipment/Titrator.md)

### Preparing the Titrator - Flush and Calibration
Reference [Titrator SOP](Equipment/Titrator.md)

- Lower the burette tip that dispenses reagent (HCL) is in a waste tube to prevent splash back. To do this highlight LIFT POSITION on the compact sample changer then press OK. This will move the device into WORK POSITION.
  
![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/da7cfad1-2f1f-4c9b-8493-885e32bfb807)

- To move the rack position, highlight RACK POSITION, then choose PREVIOUS or NEXT at the bottom of the screen using the L & R arrows and OK.

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e8a56f5e-18f3-4985-8afe-44ba803fbf0d)

- The system will push reagent through the system and clear the air bubbles. Flick the tubes gently to dislodge bubbles; ensure tubes are completely clear of bubbles.

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d9e21013-7b97-4d2a-909f-efda7b59f60d)

Once the system has flushed with reagent and all air bubbles are removed, CAREFULLY remove the cap on the pH probe and open the port on the side of the probe. 

Make sure the probe is positioned above the stirrer. If it is not the stirrer will not function properly. 

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/144e7c2d-7602-42e7-9f0b-a967556514bc)

  - The solution within the probe should be above 50mL.
  - Place a tube with fresh DI in position 12 (triangle). On the compact sample changer move the probe to position 12 using RACK POSITION. Toggle the rack using NEXT or PREVIOUS at the bottom of the screen by pressing the L & R arrows or OK.
  - Go to LIFT POSITION to change the position of the probe. Press OK to change positions.  

CALIBRATION: Place pH 7 buffer position 1 and pH 4 buffer in position of the auto sampler. Go to sample table. 
- Ensure the probe is fully submerged in the buffer, or it will misread. 

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3460a452-a9de-4ca7-b3ec-041ebf712ffe)

- If needed, clear the sample table using the 2nd program from the bottom on the right side.

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c4e664b5-6857-4188-a1a4-f0efd6025cfe)

- Insert new sample using the 3rd program from the bottom. A new sample will be added below the highlighted portion of the table.

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d8a8bda1-dd39-4fd8-8e62-67a0de6620f0)

- To delete a sample from the table, highlight the one you want to delete and then press the red x on the right side.

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6615c4be-eadd-435f-b631-2af52bb57a5d)

- Add two samples to the table. Click the edit button. Click the box under METHOD NAME and change it to CAL_pH for both samples.

|![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/501f2670-5099-4a13-a39d-ec0ee7891cc3)|![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6677222b-5d76-4558-8157-58b05f5ea050)|
|:--:|:--:|

  - Calibration will require 1 or 2 sample slots on the sample table. See *NOTES
- The probe on the compact sampler should already be in DI on rack position 12 and in the work position.

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc7664f7-140b-4b0c-9c2a-ad2d273de172)

  - On the compact sample changer METHOD should be SINGLE DIP. To change this highlight METHOD and press OK.
  - Press START to access the SAMPLE SERIES. Input number of samples as 2 and the next sample position to 1. Press START to begin calibration.
  - The calibration method should load on the titrator, and a live status table will pop up. The compact sample changer will automatically rinse in DI and move the probe to the next buffer. (NOTE: the probe does hold DI water, if needed dry the probe with a KIM wipe).

If the buffer takes more than 11s it is misreading. Stop the reading and restart the calibration

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ddb74168-af59-4dc8-83ee-aabdcdbd3be8)

  - A slope between 95% and 100% is good. If the slope is not within this range, then the calibration solutions need to be replaced and repeat the calibration with fresh solutions.

*NOTES: The Compact Sample Changer and Eco Titrator are not made to sync together. This can cause miscommunication between the two machines resulting in a procedure change. The titrator may need 1 or 2 sample slots on the table to run the calibration.
