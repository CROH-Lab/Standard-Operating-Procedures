# RNALater protocol

## Prepared by: Coral Reef and Ocean Health Research Group
## Last updated: January 28, 2025
## Pre-requisite SOP: NA
## Safety precautions: **Required PPE - <ins> enclosed shoes, gloves </ins>**

### Purpose: 
Preparing and mixing the chemicals needed to make 1L of RNALater for stable tissue storage for downstream RNA analyses. 

----

### Contents:

[Materials](#Materials)

[Procedure](#Procedure)

----

<a name="Materials"></a> 
### <ins>Materials:</ins>
- Ammonium Sulfate
- Sodium Citrate (1M)
- EDTA (0.5M)
- Sterile Distilled Water
- 1M Sulfuric Acid (for pH adjustment)
- pH meter
- Magnetic stir plate and stir bar
- Volumetric flask
- Graduated cylinder
- Filter paper (0.2 micron)

----

<a name="Procedure"></a> 
### <ins>Procedure:</ins>

1. Prepare Stock Solutions

- Sodium Citrate (1M): 
  - Dissolve 29.4 grams of sodium citrate dihydrate in 100 mL of sterile distilled water.

- EDTA (0.5M):
  - Dissolve 18.61 grams of EDTA disodium salt dihydrate in 100 mL of sterile distilled water. Adjust the pH to 8.0 with NaOH.

- In a clean volumetric flask, combine:
  - 40 mL of 0.5M EDTA
  - 25 mL of 1M Sodium Citrate
  - 700 grams of Ammonium Sulfate
    - Add sterile distilled water to the desired final volume (e.g., 1 liter).

2. Place the flask on a magnetic stir plate and stir vigorously until the Ammonium Sulfate is completely dissolved. This may require gentle heating with a hot plate, but avoid excessive heat.

3. Use 1M Acetic Acid to carefully adjust the pH of the solution to 5.2. Monitor the pH with a pH meter.

4. Filter the solution through a 0.2-micron filter to remove any particulates.

5. _(Optional)_ If desired, autoclave the filtered solution at 121ºC and 15 psi for 15-20 minutes to sterilize it.

6. Store the prepared RNALater solution at room temperature or to -80ºC.
