<a name = "Preparation"></a> 
### <ins>Preparation</ins>
- Fold filter papers into fourths to create a cone shape, weigh, and write the weight on each side of the filter in pencil
- Set up 6 jars or beakers with funnels on top

![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1c149b42-d105-42a0-9cbc-2bff826a21bd)

- Label a set of 6, PRE-WEIGHED filter papers with the site name, and numbered 1-4, 5a, and 5b
- For example, if the site name is “F3b,” then all filters papers will say “F3b 1,” “F3b 2,” “F3b 3,” “F3b 4,” “F3b 5a,” and “F3b 5b.” Then it should have the mass of filter paper after it.
- Numbers 1-5 on filter papers correspond to grain size as follows:
  - 1- Gravel
  - 2 - Coarse
  - 3 - Medium
  - 4 - Fine
  - 5 (a&b)- Silt/Clay

 ----
 
<a name = "Sieves"></a> 
### <ins>Using the Sieves</ins>
  - Stack sieves in order from top to bottom: No. 7, 35, 60, 230, then catch pan on the bottom (largest to smallest mesh, top to bottom).

  |![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d76331b1-1ed5-4575-9ee9-f61662aea335)|![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/17a3058e-f560-471a-a4c1-fcd8774b1ee3)|
  |:---:|:---:|

- One spoonful of sediment sample is enough for processing. Make sure you mix the bag so you are collecting a good representative sample from that site (there may be some rubble on the bottom of the bag, etc.)![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/45110644-e604-475e-9c68-d09c3668a9c8)

|![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9ca572d4-21b0-42bd-9cb9-54e8a3710bfc)|![Picture1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8e9c172e-5d07-4e29-ad6e-a793517a43b7)|
|:---:|:---:|

- Use tap water to rinse sediment down through each sieve. Use a squirt bottle with tap water to rinse the sediment retained on each sieve into its respective filter. Rinse down sediment to the bottom of each filter, then lay it on a tray to dry. Once dried, filters must be weighed three times on three different days.

----

<a name = "Tips"></a> 
### <ins>Tips and Reminders</ins>
- Check the catch pan once or twice during processing to make sure you are not overflowing it
- Don’t stack the sieves too tightly on top of each other, may be difficult to break loose causing spills
- Make sure to write filter masses in notebook and site name
- Remember to check off bag and checklist when done processing grain size and keep together in same area!

<a name = "Procedure"></a> 
### <ins>Procedure-specific QA/QC Methods:</ins>

_Equipment Maintenance_

All sieves will be properly cleaned and dried before and after use to prevent deterioration of the sieve and thereby inaccurate separation of the size fractions. The electronic balance will be properly calibrated and cleaned before and after each use to ensure accurate measures of mass. Further, no wet items will be weighed directly on the scale as water can damage the balance.

_Labeling_

All labels will be made with a ball point pen or sharpie to prevent bleeding. Filters and beaker masses will be recorded on both the item itself as well as within a notebook to prevent confusion.
